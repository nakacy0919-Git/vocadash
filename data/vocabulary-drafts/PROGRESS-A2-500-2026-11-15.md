# A2 vocabulary expansion — 2026-11-15

## Newly authored

500 previously undrafted A2 headwords, from **abroad** through **joke** in the master order, have been added in two internal 250-record batches. Each record has a preserved master word ID, a sense ID, part of speech, Japanese meaning, original English definition, two original English sentences with Japanese translations, an English collocation and a Japanese usage note.

There are 105 nonempty contextual synonym-candidate fields. The other 395 records have explicit notes that synonyms are not assigned; these notes must not be mistaken for reviewed decisions that no synonym exists. Candidate synonyms also require editorial review. They include restrictions such as differing grammar, formality and regional use.

The pass covers common learner confusions, including advice/advise, amused/amusing, beside/besides, during/while, few/a few and hard/hardly. Regional equivalents and spellings are noted where useful. The outdated term handicapped is presented through metalinguistic examples and a usage note rather than as a recommended contemporary label for people.

Two derived example exports and two definition/synonym exports are included. All entries remain **draft-unreviewed**. Image hints are text only; no images have been generated. Headword levels are inherited from the master and are not claims about the difficulty of each example sentence.

## Checks and limits

- `node tools/validate-vocabulary.mjs`: zero errors, zero warnings. Checks include counts, required fields, English/Japanese text presence, unique sense/example IDs, exact duplicate sentences and current derived-export correspondence.
- Reference audit: zero word-ID/headword mismatches.
- Target-word exposure check across the new records found only the expected inflection **bury → buried**, which was checked against the examples.
- The sentence hash index now covers all 53 batches.
- Editorial review is still pending across the entire dataset. Draft coverage does not mean every sense is covered or every synonym is interchangeable.
- 53 older headwords still lack a synonym candidate or a synonym-status note; the coverage report retains that separate backlog.
