## Limits of evidence
- This is **modern spelling/morphology and word-usage teaching**. In particular `-ing` may be a participle, gerund, adjective, or lexicalized noun. None of these records claim dictionary-verified historical etymology.
- The source vocabulary and even its bilingual examples are themselves editorial drafts. Master-sourced is a provenance statement, **not expert verification**.
- Morphology, semantic shifts, part of speech, source example suitability and Japanese translations need linguistic review before student use.
- There is no runtime VocaRoots/VocaUniverse UI wiring, original master edits, merge into main, or manual production deployment.
