## Teaching and provenance safeguards
- `-ed` and `-ing` categories are marked **wordform/grammar/usage**, not historical root claims; `phrase` rows are explicitly phrase-use comparisons, not etymology.
- The selected word-family relationship is a pedagogical candidate. Compound meaning, polysemy, base sense matching, and history still require individual editorial review.
- Original vocabulary examples and their translations are **also draft materials**; source evidence does not establish professional linguistic verification.
- The selection is not a production feed and does not modify existing VocaDash master vocabulary, live UI, deployment settings, or published content.
